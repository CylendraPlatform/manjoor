# Art direction — premium rebuild

## Why the previous homepage looked amateurish

- Portrait phone photos were stretched as 16:9 full-bleed heroes.
- Successive slides were the same product family at different zooms.
- The whole site collapsed into an eight-card bakarat grid.
- Brown overlays and identical rounded cards repeated in every band.

## Specification

Editorial split hero, not a billboard crop.

| Slide | Tone | Asset | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production | Cream | `production.jpg` — real product `(3)` in a studio frame | Workshop photograph |
| Craft | Charcoal | `craft.jpg` lathe close-up | Conceptual illustration |
| Custom | Linen | `custom.jpg` interior still | Conceptual illustration |

Homepage sequence: capabilities (typography) → four real products (editorial, not a uniform grid) → craft story (dark) → three labeled concepts → wholesale band → three steps → closing CTA.

## Asset limitations

No background-removal library is installed. Real products keep authentic shop context inside a cream editorial frame; they are not isolated cut-outs. A studio reshoot of bakarat on seamless would replace `production.jpg`.
